Privacy notice

Yutdugi needs no account and no sign-up. Here is exactly what information goes where. 한국어

In one line

There is no sign-up or login, and the operator does not collect or store personal information such as names or contact details on any server. No ads, no analytics, no tracking cookies.

1. What is handled and why

InformationWhere and whyHow long
Nickname (if you type one)Sent in real time to the other people in the same online room so they can see who they are playing. It is not stored on a server.Gone when the game ends
Nickname (on your device)Saved only in your own browser (localStorage) so you don’t have to type it again.Until you clear your browser data
Room code, temporary ID, movesExchanged only through the real-time relay and kept in your browser tab (sessionStorage) so players in the same room stay in sync and can continue after a refresh. These are random values that do not identify you.Gone when you close the tab

Playing against the computer or with two players on one device runs entirely on your device.

2. Third-party services

The services below are needed to deliver the page and relay online games. While you connect, each service may keep technical logs such as your IP address, browser information and access time under its own policy.

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA) — hosts the web page
  • Supabase (Supabase, Inc., servers in Seoul, Korea) — relays online games in real time
  • Google Fonts (Google LLC, USA) — provides the typefaces
  • jsDelivr — delivers the relay library file

The operator does not receive these logs or pass them to anyone.

3. Sharing and selling

We do not provide or sell personal information to any person or company. The only exception would be a request made under applicable law.

4. Deleting your information

Clearing this site’s data in your browser settings removes the saved nickname immediately. Nothing about you remains on a server to delete.

5. Children

We do not ask for age or collect any information. We recommend not using a real name or contact details as a nickname.
